The rollout built the room before the doors opened.

Project Unsanctioned was a staged rave campaign for OSTAR × House Bl3nd. Film, portraiture and short-form edits built the world first; Arcade Rave: Gotta Gacha was the payoff.

Planning began June 16. The party-room unit shot July 23, with additional production and post continuing through the August rollout. I co-directed the hero film, then edited and colored the campaign across multiple cameras and formats.

The rollout moved from a fictionalized party reveal to a packed room at the Russell Industrial Center in Detroit on August 21, and the teaser crossed 100K Instagram impressions.

Build the world. Cut what it doesn’t need.

The original plan included a parking-lot sequence before the party. We shot and graded it, then removed it when the film worked better by dropping straight into Scene 2.

The rooftop footage still carries the campaign’s visual identity, which is why it opens this page. The released film itself is the party-room cut.
