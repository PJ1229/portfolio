00 / CAMPAIGN BRIEF JUN—AUG 2026

ONE MONTH.
FOUR DROPS.
ONE FULL ROOM.

Project Unsanctioned was a staged rave campaign for OSTAR × House Bl3nd. Film, portraiture and short-form edits built the world first; Arcade Rave: Gotta Gacha was the payoff.

Planning began June 16. The party-room unit shot July 23, with additional production and post continuing through the August rollout. I co-directed the hero film, then edited and colored the campaign across multiple cameras and formats.
