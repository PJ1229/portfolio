  7. How it came together

    Our first meeting was June 16, and we met again June 30, July 10, and July 26, with the full logistics meeting on August 12. Production started August 1 and ran through the night of the rave.

    There's a sound-design breakdown that goes through the SFX, and a separate tutorial on the color grade.

    The teaser that went out three days before the rave was cut from Anthony's a7III footage (S-Log2) and graded to match the campaign's black-and-white look.
