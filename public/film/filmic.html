  2. WHY YOU? — 250 WORD LIMIT

    My entire life I thought I'd be an engineer. I spent all of high school taking advanced math and engineering courses and even worked as a software engineer: from developing full stack apps for my local library in high school then automating workflows helping companies like Tesla, Rivian, and Ford at an automotive supplier for the last 2 years. However, what started as a side-hobby evolved into my entire life. Film became my way of expression, and I was surprisingly able to create an impact on other people through it. This mental divide inspires me to find the harmony between creativity and logic as I continue to pursue both paths in conjunction with each other. I believe this fresh perspective of someone coming from an unconventional path towards the film industry would benefit the Filmic community.

  4. ROLE — EDITOR · 250 WORD LIMIT

    I'm most drawn to editor. My favorite part of making a film is sorting through the footage and putting a bunch of disconnected pieces together. I love the small details like the creative choices you need to make when there's a missing scene or one of the scenes was shot differently than intended. A really big part of what got me into film in the first place was color grading. Being able to grade any log footage I shoot on my camera and turn it into something that looks straight out of the movies is extremely addicting, and I grade by hand on DaVinci Resolve (No LUTS!). I love replicating scenes of my favorite movies and analyzing how the color shapes the scene. One of my favorite examples of color-grading in film is in La La Land where the colors tell you the entire story from falling in love to letting go. Combined with VFX and sound design, the edit is where an idea stops being footage and becomes the thing you actually meant to make. That's the part I want to get much better at, and getting constructive criticism on a much more experienced team like FILMIC would help me grow.

  5. CRITIQUE — SMITTEN: FIRST DATE SERENADE · 200 WORD LIMIT

    Here's a critique of the first 15 seconds of "Smitten: First Date Serenade". If this is a short form advertisement (assuming so since it's shot vertically), the hook needs to be a lot more attention grabbing. Ways you could do this are to make the camera do a fast zoom into the background character or adding a text bubble VFX pop up tied to the phone. Additionally, I'm noticing how the color is completely flat — to make it more stylized I'd add a more dreamy color-grade to sell the app's aesthetic. A quick fix for the slow feel of this short form advert would be to add more dramatic transitions and SFX. The SFX also feels extremely artificial; I would add a parametric equalizer to mesh it more with the background music. Also, the overlay of the app looks like someone just added a photo over the video instead of embedding it within it. I would create a transparent overlay of the app UI in Figma, export it into After Effects and turn it 3D using the camera, tie it to the phone so it matches the movement, add glow, lower the opacity, and add gaussian blur, then add apple-esque smooth animations when going through the app.
