Going back to before they knew each other.

I wrote, directed, shot, and graded this on Belle Isle in August 2026. It’s inspired by Call Me by Your Name, using dialogue from the scene where Elio talks with his father after Oliver leaves.

The picture plays in reverse, as if stepping back to before the two people knew each other. It’s scored with that dialogue and Ichiko Aoba’s “Dawn in the Adan.”

Three looks: afternoon, dusk, ember.

I graded three passes in DaVinci Resolve, from afternoon light through dusk into a darker ember. The cut is in Premiere.

Afternoon. Blacks lifted into a warm olive and the midtones pushed toward dry-grass yellow without letting the skin go sallow. The sky is held a half-step under the field so the light stays behind them.

Dusk. Highlights graded amber and the blue in the water pulled back toward grey. Skin is kept a step warmer than the scene so the two figures separate from the sea instead of sinking into it.

Ember. Chroma concentrated in the red-orange band and everything else desaturated toward near-black. The sky is left to fall off into the ember rather than corrected to neutral.
